# How to Play

Every game shares the same layout:

- **Top bar (HUD)**: the **MENU** button (or **Esc**) on the left, the game name, and live stats on the right (chips, score, time, books…).
- **The table**: the felt (green by default, or any colour from Customize) where cards are dealt, moved and flipped.
- **Controls**: buttons along the bottom or side. Each shows its keyboard shortcut in the corner.
- **Pause menu (Esc)**: Resume, **How to Play** (opens the rulebook at that game's chapter), New Game, Main Menu.

## Main menu keys

The menu has two pages. Click the arrows at the sides, the page dots, or press **← / →** or **Tab** to switch.

- **The Classics**: **1** Blackjack, **2** Solitaire, **3** Go Fish.
- **More Games**: **1** Texas Hold'em, **2** Crazy Eights, **3** War, **4** Spider.

**O** Options, **C** Customize, **P** Your profile, **R** Rulebook, **F** Fullscreen (Q quits in the desktop app). When you leave a game, the menu returns to the page you came from.

## Your profile

Click your name badge in the top-left corner of the main menu, or press **P**, to edit your profile:

- **Name**: just type (up to 10 letters, numbers, spaces, dots or dashes). Backspace deletes. Leave it empty to show as YOU.
- **Look**: use the arrows to change skin tone, hair style and colour, hat and hat colour, facial hair (mustache or beard) and its colour, eyes (glasses, shades, monocle, eyepatch), an extra (earring, freckles, rose), shirt colour and backdrop. **RANDOM LOOK** rolls a new one.

Your portrait and name appear on your seat at every Club table: Blackjack, Hold'em, Go Fish, Crazy Eights and War.

## Customize

Press **CUSTOMIZE** (or **C**) on the main menu. Your choices are saved and used in every game.

- **Table colour**: Green (default), Blue, Red, Brown, Orange, Pink, Purple, Teal or Charcoal.
- **Deck**: **Classic** (crisp white cards with royal court cards) or **Western**, a worn 1890s deck with yellowed card stock, coffee stains, faded ink, nicked corners and creases. Its court cards are frontier characters in wanted-poster frames: an outlaw Jack in a bandana, a saloon Queen in a plumed hat and a cattle-baron King with a handlebar moustache. The Ace of Spades carries an 1890 maker's stamp.
- **Card back**: four backs for each deck. Classic: Crimson, Cobalt, Forest, Royal. Western: Oxblood, Prussian, Sage, Tobacco, each with a sheriff-star medallion.

The live preview shows your table, deck and back together.

## The rulebook

Press **RULEBOOK** (or **R**) on the main menu to open the in-game book. Click the right page or press **→** to turn forward, click the left page or press **←** to turn back, and use the coloured tabs on the right to jump to a chapter: START, DECK, BJ, POKER, SOL, SPDR, FISH, CRAZY, WAR, CLUB and REAL (the real-world rules each game is based on, with house rules). **Esc** closes the book and takes you back where you were.

## Blackjack

**You vs the dealer.** The dealer's moves are fixed by casino rules, so the challenge is your own decisions.

### Table-mates
Before the first round you can seat **none, 1 or 2 Club members** at the table (press **SEATS / T** between rounds to change them). Table-mates bet and play their own hands with their own chips, in their own style (see [The Club](#the-club)). They don't change your odds: everyone plays against the dealer, never against each other. Turn order is right seat, you, left seat, then the dealer. Their chip stacks are saved between sessions.

### Mood, nerve and bets
Table-mates don't play the same way all night. Each one tracks their **streak** (shown as W3 or L2 on their panel) and their stack compared with when they sat down, and that sets their **mood**: HOT, ON FIRE, COLD, TILT, SHAKY, UP BIG or DOWN BAD.

- **Bets change with the run of cards.** Each member has a betting temper. Some **press** a winning streak, some **chase** losses with bigger bets, and some back off when cold. Players who are well up also bet more. BYTE always bets 3% of its stack.
- **Nerve changes how they play.** Hotheads play more dangerously when losing (hitting stiff hands, doubling more). Jittery members get cautious and freeze on 12–16. Confident members get bolder on a hot streak. Steady members play the same win or lose.
- **They talk about it.** Members announce big bet changes ("UPPING IT TO 90. IT HAS TO TURN."), complain on a cold run ("THIS SUCKS."), celebrate streaks and comebacks, and react when the dealer busts or you get a blackjack. Each member has their own lines mixed in with the general table talk.

### Goal
Finish with a hand closer to **21** than the dealer's without going over.

- Number cards are worth their value.
- **J, Q and K** are worth **10**.
- An **Ace** is worth **11**, or **1** if 11 would put you over 21. A hand with an Ace counted as 11 is called **soft** (for example, "SOFT 17").

### A round
1. **Bet.** Click the chips at the bottom (5, 25, 100, 500), or press **1–4**. **CLEAR (C)** removes the bet and **ALL IN (A)** bets everything. Your last bet is kept for the next round.
2. **Deal (Space / Enter).** You get two face-up cards. The dealer gets one face-up card and one face-down "hole" card.
3. If the dealer shows an Ace or a 10-value card, they **peek** for blackjack first.
4. **Play your hand:**
   | Button | Key | What it does |
   |---|---|---|
   | HIT | H | Take another card |
   | STAND | S | Keep your total and end the hand |
   | DOUBLE | D | Double your bet, take exactly one more card, then stand. Only on your first two cards. |
   | SPLIT | P | Two cards of the same value become two separate hands, each with its own bet. You can split up to 4 hands. Split Aces get one card each. |
   | HINT | ? | The **advisor** shows the mathematically best move (basic strategy) |
5. **Dealer's turn.** The hole card is turned over. The dealer **must hit on 16 or less and stands on all 17s**.
6. **Payouts:**
   | Result | Pays |
   |---|---|
   | Win | 1 to 1 (bet 25, win 25) |
   | Blackjack (Ace + 10-value on the first two cards) | 3 to 2 (bet 20, win 30) |
   | Push (tie) | Your bet is returned |
   | Bust (over 21) or lower total | You lose your bet |

The **shoe** (top right) holds 1–6 decks (set in Options). It's reshuffled automatically when about 75% has been dealt. You start with **1,000 chips**. If you run out, you can take a fresh stack.

### Tips
- Always split Aces and 8s. Never split 10s or 5s.
- Double down on 11 against anything but an Ace.
- Stand on hard 12–16 when the dealer shows 2–6 (with 12, stand only against 4–6), since the dealer is likely to bust.
- If unsure, press **?**.

## Texas Hold'em

No-limit Texas Hold'em as a **sit-and-go** against 1 to 3 Club members. Everyone starts with 1000 chips. Lose them all and you're out; the last player with chips wins.

### Setup

Pick 1–3 opponents and the blinds: **RISING** (10/20, going up every 10 hands to 500/1000) or **FIXED 10/20**.

### A hand

1. The two players left of the button post the **small blind** and **big blind**. With two players, the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.
2. Everyone gets two face-down **hole cards**. Betting starts left of the big blind.
3. **Flop**: three shared cards, then a betting round. **Turn**: a fourth card and a betting round. **River**: a fifth card and the last betting round. A card is burned before each.
4. **Showdown**: the best five-card hand from your two cards plus the five on the board wins. If everyone else folds, you win without showing.

### Betting controls

| Button | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|
| FOLD | F | Give up the hand |
| CHECK / CALL | C | Check when nothing is owed, otherwise match the bet |
| − / + | | Change the raise size |
| BET / RAISE TO | R | Bet or raise to the amount shown |
| MIN, 1/2 POT, POT | | Quick sizes |
| ALL IN | A | Bet every chip |
| ODDS | O | Toggle the odds helper (your chance to win vs the share of the pot you need to call) |

No-limit rules: a raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise; an all-in that is too small to be a full raise does not reopen the betting; an all-in player can only win what they matched, and the rest goes to a **side pot**. Ties split the pot, and an odd chip goes to the first winner left of the button.

### Hand rankings (best to worst)

Royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. Suits never break ties. 5-4-3-2-A is the lowest straight, and straights don't wrap around (Q-K-A-2-3 is not a straight).

### How the Club plays poker

Each member has a poker style: **Shark** (tight and aggressive, reads pot odds), **LAG** (loose and aggressive), **Rock** (only strong hands), **Station** (calls almost anything), **Maniac** (raises and bluffs all the time) or **Wild** (changes gear every hand). They estimate their hand's strength by simulating the rest of the deal, and their nerve changes how they play after big wins and losses: hotheads go **ON TILT**, jittery players get **RATTLED**, and winners get **ON A HEATER**.

## Solitaire (Klondike)

**A solo puzzle.** Sort the whole deck into four suit piles.

### The layout
- **Stock** (top left): face-down cards. Click it to turn cards onto the **waste** pile next to it.
- **Foundations** (top right, four slots marked "A"): build each suit **up** from Ace to King.
- **Tableau** (seven columns): build **down** in **alternating colours**, for example red 9 on black 10.

### Rules
- You can move the top card of the waste, any face-up card on the tableau (it carries every card on top of it), or the top card of a foundation.
- Only a **King** (or a run starting with a King) can go into an empty column.
- A face-down tableau card turns over automatically once it's uncovered.
- **Draw 1** turns one card at a time; **Draw 3** turns three, and only the top one is playable. Choose in **Options**.
- When the stock is empty, click its slot to **recycle** the waste back into the stock.

### Controls
| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Move cards | **Drag** them onto a legal pile |
| Quick move | **Click** a card to send it to the best legal spot (foundation first) |
| Draw | Click the stock |
| NEW DEAL | N |
| UNDO | Z (unlimited) |
| HINT | H: flashes a suggested move |
| AUTO | A: appears once every card is face up and finishes the game for you |

### Scoring
| Move | Points |
|---|---|
| Waste → tableau | +5 |
| Any card → foundation | +10 |
| Turning over a tableau card | +5 |
| Foundation → tableau | −15 |
| Recycling the stock (Draw 1) | −100 |
| Time bonus on a win (over 30 s) | 700,000 ÷ seconds |

Win and the cards bounce across the screen, just like the original. Click to continue.

## Spider Solitaire

Two decks, ten columns. Build eight complete runs from King down to Ace in a single suit; each finished run leaves the table.

- **Deal**: 54 cards, six in each of the first four columns and five in the other six, with only the top card face up. The other 50 cards are the stock.
- **Suits** (chosen in the side panel for the next game): **1 suit** (all spades), **2 suits** (spades and hearts) or **4 suits**.
- **Moving**: any card can go on a card one rank higher, in any suit. A group of cards can only move together if it's one suit and in order. Any card or run can go in an empty column.
- **Deal row** (D): one card onto every column. Every column needs at least one card first.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Drag or click | Move a card or run (click picks the best spot) |
| Z | Undo |
| H | Hint (press again for the next one) |
| D | Deal a row |
| N | New game |

**Scoring** (Windows style): start at 500, −1 for every move, deal and undo, +100 for every finished run.

## Go Fish

Play **free-for-all** against 1–3 Club members, or **teams 2v2** with a partner. Pick the mode and players on the setup screen: click a seat, then click a Club member in the grid (or press **RANDOM** to fill the empty seats).

### Goal
Collect the most **books**. A book is all four cards of one rank (four 7s, four Queens…). As soon as a player holds all four, the book is laid **face up on the table** in front of them, so everyone can see the score.

### Setup
- 2–3 players: 7 cards each. 4 players: 5 cards each.
- The rest of the deck is the **pond** in the middle.
- You always go first.

### Your turn
1. **Click a card** in your hand to ask for that rank. You can only ask for ranks you already hold.
2. With more than one player you could ask, **click the player** (their panel flashes). **Right-click** cancels.
3. **If they have any**, they hand over all of them and you **go again**.
4. **If not**, they say **"GO FISH!"** and you draw from the pond. If you draw the rank you asked for, you go again ("FISHED MY WISH!"). Otherwise your turn ends.
5. **Laying down a book also earns another turn.**

If your hand runs empty you draw a card. Once the pond is empty, a player with no cards sits out. The game ends when all **13 books** are on the table.

### Teams 2v2 (house rule)
- You and your **partner** (sitting across from you) are **Team Blue**. The two **rivals** are **Team Red**. Seats alternate, so turns go Blue, Red, Blue, Red.
- You may only ask players on the **other team**. Teammates never trade cards.
- Each player lays their own books, but the **team total** decides the winner (shown top right as BLUE / RED).
- Once the pond is empty, if a team holds all four of a rank between its two players, they **pool** them into a book. This stops the game from getting stuck.

### The HUD
- **Player panels** (top): portrait, name, card count, team, skill hearts and number of books.
- **Books on the table**: each player's books sit under their panel. Yours are on the left.
- **Speech bubbles**: who asked whom for what, "Go Fish!", and each member's own lines.
- **Table talk** (right): a running log of every ask, give, draw and book. Read it to work out who holds what.
- **Pond** count (centre).

### Tips
- When someone asks for a rank, they're holding at least one. Ask them for it on your next turn.
- If you ask someone for a rank and they say "Go Fish", don't ask them again until they've drawn more cards.
- In teams, watch what your rivals ask your partner for. Your partner can't give you cards, but the rival who asked is now a good target.

## Crazy Eights

Be the first to play all your cards. Play against 1–3 Club members.

- **Deal**: 7 cards each with two players, 5 each with three or four. One card starts the play pile; if it's an 8 it's buried in the stock.
- **Your turn**: play a card that matches the top card's **suit or rank**. **8s are wild**: play one any time and name the next suit (click a suit or press 1–4).
- **Can't play?** Click the stock or press **D** to draw until you can. You may also draw when you could play. When the stock runs out, the play pile (except the top card) is shuffled into a new stock. **PASS** appears only when you can't play and nothing is left to draw.
- **Scoring**: the first player out collects points for the cards left in every other hand: 8 = 50, K/Q/J/10 = 10, Ace = 1, other cards = face value.
- **Modes**: **ONE HAND**, or **MATCH TO 100** (play hands until someone reaches 100 points).
- If nobody can play and nothing can be drawn, the hand is blocked and the lowest hand wins it.

Easy members play at random. Normal members save their 8s and play their longest suit. Hard members also remember which suits each player couldn't follow and dump high cards when someone is about to go out.

## War

Pure luck. Win all the cards. Play against 1–3 Club members.

- **Deal**: the deck is split evenly: 26 each for two players, 17 for three (one card set aside), 13 for four.
- **Battle**: everyone flips their top card. The highest takes them all. Aces are high.
- **War**: if the top cards tie, the tied players each put one card face down and one face up; the highest new card takes the whole pile. With three or four players, everyone joins the war. If you run out during a war, your last card stays face up and counts for you.
- **Modes**: **FULL GAME** (until one player has everything) or **ONE PASS** (go through the deck once; most cards won wins).

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| FLIP (Space, or click your deck) | Flip the next card |
| AUTO (A) | Flip automatically |
| SPEED (S) | ×1, ×3 or ×8 |

## The Club

The Club is 18 computer players. Each one has a portrait, a usual skill level (Easy, Normal or Hard), a Go Fish habit, a Blackjack style, a poker style and their own table talk. Your win/loss record against each member is saved and shown in the picker and the rulebook.

### Form: hearts are dealt each game

A member's skill level is only how they usually play. When a game starts, each member at the table gets their **form** for that game, shown as hearts on their seat panel (1 heart = off night, 2 = steady, 3 = sharp). Usual skill sets the odds, but anything can happen:

| Usually | 1 heart | 2 hearts | 3 hearts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 60% | 30% | 10% |
| Normal | 25% | 50% | 25% |
| Hard | 10% | 30% | 60% |

The AI plays to its form, not its reputation. A Hard member on an off night forgets what was asked in Go Fish, throws away 8s in Crazy Eights and calls too loosely in Hold'em. An Easy member on a sharp night remembers asks and plays the odds. In Blackjack, a sharp table-mate plays closer to perfect strategy and an off-night table-mate sometimes hits or stands by mistake. The table talk tells you when someone is looking sharp or off their game. War is pure luck, so nobody gets hearts there. Hearts aren't shown in the picker: you find out when you sit down.
