# Rules Sources

Pixel Card Club follows published rules for its three games. This page lists the sources each game is based on, and every place where the game uses its own house rule.

## Blackjack

**Based on:** [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Blackjack](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/blackjack)

- Card values, the dealer's hole card, hit, stand, double down, splitting pairs, and 3:2 for a natural follow the Bicycle rules.
- The dealer hits on 16 or less and stands on all 17s.

**The HINT advisor** uses multi-deck basic strategy for "dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed". You can compare it against these charts:

- [Blackjack Info: Basic Strategy Engine (2 decks, S17, DAS)](https://www.blackjackinfo.com/blackjack-basic-strategy-engine/?numdecks=2&soft17=s17&das=yes)
- [JackAce: Multi-deck S17 strategy chart](https://www.jackace.com/gambling/blackjack/systems/basic-strategy/charts/MD_S17/)
- [Blackjack Review: Basic strategy](https://www.blackjackreview.com/wp/encyclopedia/blackjack-basic-strategy/)

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Insurance | Not offered |
| Surrender | Not offered |
| Splitting | Up to 4 hands. Split Aces get one card each. |
| Table-mates | Club members play their own hands against the dealer, in their own style, and never split |

## Solitaire (Klondike)

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Klondike](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/klondike/)
- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Solitaire](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/solitaire)
- [Wikipedia: Klondike (solitaire)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike_(solitaire)), which covers draw 1 and draw 3, and Windows-style scoring

The layout (7 tableau columns, 4 foundations, stock and waste), building down in alternating colours, building foundations up from Ace, and Kings only in empty columns all follow these rules.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Passes through the stock | Unlimited (Draw 1 recycles cost 100 points) |
| Scoring | Windows-style standard scoring with a time bonus |
| Undo, hints, auto-finish | Video-game conveniences, not part of the card game |

## Go Fish

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Go Fish](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/go-fish): 7 cards each for 2–3 players and 5 each for 4 or more. You must hold the rank you ask for. The player asked gives all their cards of that rank. A completed book is laid face up and the player goes again. The game ends when all 13 books are made.
- [Pagat: Go Fish](https://www.pagat.com/quartet/gofish.html): a closely related version where the game ends as soon as a player's hand or the stock runs out.
- [Pagat: Go Fish variations](https://www.pagat.com/invented/gofish_vars.html)

Pixel Card Club follows the Bicycle version, including the extra turn for a book and playing on until all 13 books are down.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Teams 2v2 | Our own variant. Partners sit across from each other, may only ask the other team, and add their books together. Published Go Fish rules don't include a team mode. [Pagat's Pokefish](https://www.pagat.com/invented/pokefish.html) is an example of an invented Go Fish variant played in partnerships. |
| Team pooling | Once the pond is empty, a team holding all four cards of a rank between its players pools them into a book, so a game can't get stuck |
| Empty hand | Draw a card from the pond. If the pond is empty, sit out. |
