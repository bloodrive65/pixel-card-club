# Rules Sources

Pixel Card Club follows published rules for its seven games. This page lists the sources each game is based on, and every place where the game uses its own house rule.

## Blackjack

**Based on:** [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Blackjack](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/blackjack)

- Card values, the dealer's hole card, hit, stand, double down, splitting pairs, and 3:2 for a natural follow the Bicycle rules.
- The dealer hits on 16 or less and stands on all 17s.

**The HINT advisor** uses multi-deck basic strategy for "dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed". You can compare it against these charts:

- [Blackjack Info: Basic Strategy Engine (2 decks, S17, DAS)](https://www.blackjackinfo.com/blackjack-basic-strategy-engine/?numdecks=2&soft17=s17&das=yes)
- [JackAce: Multi-deck S17 strategy chart](https://www.jackace.com/gambling/blackjack/systems/basic-strategy/charts/MD_S17/)
- [Blackjack Review: Basic strategy](https://www.blackjackreview.com/wp/encyclopedia/blackjack-basic-strategy/)

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Insurance | Not offered |
| Surrender | Not offered |
| Splitting | Up to 4 hands. Split Aces get one card each. |
| Table-mates | Club members play their own hands against the dealer, in their own style, and never split |

## Solitaire (Klondike)

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Klondike](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/klondike/)
- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Solitaire](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/solitaire)
- [Wikipedia: Klondike (solitaire)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike_(solitaire)), which covers draw 1 and draw 3, and Windows-style scoring

The layout (7 tableau columns, 4 foundations, stock and waste), building down in alternating colours, building foundations up from Ace, and Kings only in empty columns all follow these rules.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Passes through the stock | Unlimited (Draw 1 recycles cost 100 points) |
| Scoring | Windows-style standard scoring with a time bonus |
| Undo, hints, auto-finish | Video-game conveniences, not part of the card game |

## Go Fish

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Go Fish](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/go-fish): 7 cards each for 2–3 players and 5 each for 4 or more. You must hold the rank you ask for. The player asked gives all their cards of that rank. A completed book is laid face up and the player goes again. The game ends when all 13 books are made.
- [Pagat: Go Fish](https://www.pagat.com/quartet/gofish.html): a closely related version where the game ends as soon as a player's hand or the stock runs out.
- [Pagat: Go Fish variations](https://www.pagat.com/invented/gofish_vars.html)

Pixel Card Club follows the Bicycle version, including the extra turn for a book and playing on until all 13 books are down.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Teams 2v2 | Our own variant. Partners sit across from each other, may only ask the other team, and add their books together. Published Go Fish rules don't include a team mode. [Pagat's Pokefish](https://www.pagat.com/invented/pokefish.html) is an example of an invented Go Fish variant played in partnerships. |
| Team pooling | Once the pond is empty, a team holding all four cards of a rank between its players pools them into a book, so a game can't get stuck |
| Empty hand | Draw a card from the pond. If the pond is empty, sit out. |

## Texas Hold'em

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Texas Hold'em Poker](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/texas-holdem-poker): blinds, two hole cards, the flop, turn and river, four betting rounds and the showdown.
- [Wikipedia: Texas hold 'em](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_hold_%27em): heads-up blinds (the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop), the no-limit minimum raise, burn cards, odd chips and kickers.
- [Pagat: Poker betting](https://www.pagat.com/poker/rules/betting.html): side pots. When a player is all in, "the main pot contains your bet and equal amounts of chips from all active players", and later bets go to a separate side pot.
- [Pagat: Poker hand ranking](https://www.pagat.com/poker/rules/ranking.html): hand order, suits never break ties, 5-4-3-2-A is the lowest straight and straights do not wrap around.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Format | Sit-and-go: everyone starts with 1000 chips; last player with chips wins |
| Blinds | RISING: 10/20 going up every 10 hands to 500/1000. FIXED: always 10/20 |
| Short all-in raise | Does not reopen the betting for players who have already acted |
| Uncalled bets | Returned to the bettor |
| Odds helper | A video-game aid (press O), not part of the card game |

## Spider Solitaire

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Spider Solitaire](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/spider-solitaire): two decks, ten columns, building down regardless of suit, moving same-suit runs, and dealing a new row only when every column has a card.
- [Wikipedia: Spider (solitaire)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider_(solitaire)): the 54-card deal (six cards in the first four columns, five in the rest), the 50-card stock dealt ten at a time, the 1, 2 and 4 suit versions, and Windows scoring (start at 500, minus 1 per move including undo, plus 100 per finished suit).

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Deal | The 54-card Windows deal. Bicycle deals ten columns of five and a 54-card stock with a short last deal. |
| Undo | Allowed. Each undo costs 1 point, and undoing a finished run takes back its 100. |
| Hints | A video-game aid, not part of the card game |

## Crazy Eights

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play Crazy Eights](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/crazy-eights): five cards each, a starter that is an 8 is buried in the stock, match suit or rank, 8s are wild and name a suit, draw until you can play (you may draw even if you can play), and the winner collects 50 for each 8, 10 for each face card or 10, 1 for each ace and face value for the rest.
- [Pagat: Crazy Eights](https://www.pagat.com/eights/crazy8s.html): "five cards to each player (seven each if there are only two players)", the played cards except the top one are shuffled into a new stock when the stock runs out, and the game is blocked if every player passes.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Match to 100 | Our own option. Bicycle scores single hands with no target. |
| Blocked hand | The player with the lowest hand value wins it and collects the difference |
| Passing | Only allowed when you cannot play and nothing is left to draw |

## War

**Based on:**

- [Bicycle Cards: How to Play War](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/war): 26 cards each, the higher card takes both, and a tie means war: one card face down and one face up, repeated while the cards tie.
- [Pagat: War](https://www.pagat.com/war/war.html): Ace high; with three or four players the deck is split 17 or 13 each and "all players take part in a war"; when a player runs out during a war, their last card stays face up and is used for every battle in that war; Tod und Leben, a version played through the deck only once.

**House rules**

| Rule | Pixel Card Club |
|---|---|
| Won cards | Go under your pile in random order, which keeps games from looping forever |
| Three players | One spare card is set aside so the deal is even |
| One Pass | Based on Tod und Leben. If the last battle ties, each player keeps their own cards. |
| Game end | You see the result as soon as you run out of cards |
